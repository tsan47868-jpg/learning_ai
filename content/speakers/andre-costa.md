André Costa helps people ship their first real AI project. He started as a self-taught web developer in Lisbon, building small tools for local businesses — booking pages, inventory trackers, the unglamorous software that keeps a shop running — and discovered that he learned more from shipping imperfect work than from any course he abandoned halfway.

That habit carried into AI. When generative models became accessible, André began building practical tools on top of them: a meeting-notes assistant for a neighbourhood association, a listing generator for a family market, a support triage bot for a five-person startup. He documented each build in public, step by step, including the parts that broke.

Those write-ups led him to Atlântico Tech, where he leads developer experience — writing guides, maintaining starter templates, and running the workshops where newcomers build and deploy their first AI-powered tool in a single afternoon.

André teaches that the fastest way to learn AI is to pick a problem you actually have, build the smallest thing that helps, and improve it once it is in real use.
