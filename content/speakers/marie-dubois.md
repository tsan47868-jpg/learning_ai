Marie Dubois has spent the last decade making artificial intelligence understandable to people who think they have no business touching it. Her teaching career began in adult education, where she discovered that most AI anxiety comes not from the technology itself but from the way it is explained — too much jargon, too few examples, and no room for questions.

Before moving into AI education full time, Marie led onboarding programs at a Paris-based software company, where she built the internal training track that took non-technical staff from spreadsheets to automating their own workflows. That experience shaped her conviction: people learn AI best when they build something useful in the first week, not when they study theory for a month.

At Lumière Learning, Marie designs the foundation courses used by thousands of beginners across Europe. Her lessons are known for plain language, patient pacing, and the "explain it twice" method — once with an analogy, once with a real example.

Marie believes there is no such thing as a "non-technical person," only topics that haven't been taught clearly yet.
