Priya Sharma builds machine learning systems and, more importantly, figures out whether they actually work. Her path began in biochemistry, where she learned to distrust results she couldn't reproduce — a habit that turned out to translate perfectly into machine learning, where confident-sounding outputs can hide plain wrong answers.

After completing her master's degree in computer science, Priya joined a healthcare startup in Bengaluru as its first machine learning engineer. There she built and, crucially, evaluated the models used to prioritize patient follow-ups, discovering firsthand how a model can look excellent on a benchmark and still fail the people it was meant to serve. That work shaped her specialty: rigorous, honest evaluation of AI systems.

Today she leads the machine learning team at Banyan AI Labs, where her group builds generative AI tools for Indian languages and publishes the evaluation harnesses used to test them.

Priya teaches that the most valuable AI skill is not prompting a model well — it is knowing when its answer deserves your trust.
