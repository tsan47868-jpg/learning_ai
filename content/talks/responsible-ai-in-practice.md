Isabella Martinez replaces vague advice about "using AI responsibly" with habits you can apply on Monday morning. This session is about the practical decisions — what to feed a model, what to check, and when to keep a human firmly in the loop.

## What You'll Learn

**Privacy: What Leaves Your System**
Isabella shows how to think about what you paste into AI tools — customer data, personal details, company secrets — and simple alternatives: anonymize first, summarize instead of sharing, and know your tool's data policy before you type.

**Accuracy: Verify What Matters**
Not every output deserves the same scrutiny. Isabella introduces a risk-based check: low-stakes brainstorming needs a glance; anything factual, financial, medical, or public needs a source and a second look. You will build your own quick verification habit.

**Bias and Fairness in Real Decisions**
Using a hiring screening example, Isabella demonstrates how bias enters through data and task design, and the safeguards that catch it — diverse test cases, human review on consequential decisions, and documented criteria.

**Keep Judgment Where It Belongs**
The closing framework: automate drafting and sorting, but keep responsibility — the decision and the signature — with a person who can explain them.

## Who Should Attend

Everyone using AI in real work, especially team leads, and anyone asked to write or review an AI usage policy. No technical background required.

*"Responsible is not a paragraph in a policy. It is the small decision you make every time before you hit send."* — Isabella Martinez
