André Costa's signature session: in one afternoon you build a chatbot that answers real questions about something you care about — and deploy it where other people can use it. The goal is not a demo. It is a small, finished, useful thing.

## What You'll Learn

**Start With the Question, Not the Code**
André begins where successful projects begin: who will use this, what will they ask, and what does a good answer look like? You will write five real questions before writing any code — and those questions become your test set.

**Grounding: Giving the Bot Your Knowledge**
A general chatbot knows everything and your context. You will add your own documents or pages, keep answers tied to them, and add the honest fallback: "I don't have that information." Simple retrieval, explained without jargon.

**The Conversation Loop**
You will build the parts that make a bot feel usable: remembering the current conversation, handling a message that arrived empty, staying on topic, and knowing when to hand off to a person.

**Ship It and Iterate**
André deploys the bot so it has a real URL, then walks through what changes once strangers use it — the questions you never imagined, and how each one becomes a small improvement.

## Who Should Attend

People who have completed the foundations sessions and want a project to show for it. Some coding helps, but André provides a complete starter template either way.

*"Ship the smallest version that helps someone. Everything you learn next comes from using it."* — André Costa
