A good prompt is not magic words — it is a clear brief. In this session Marie Dubois turns prompting from superstition into a repeatable skill you can apply to any model, in any tool, starting today.

## What You'll Learn

**The Anatomy of a Prompt**
Marie breaks every effective prompt into four parts: the role you give the model, the context it needs, the task you actually want, and the shape of the output. Once you can name these parts, you stop guessing and start constructing.

**Context Is the Whole Game**
Most weak answers come from questions the model could not answer well. Marie shows how a few sentences of context — who the audience is, what you already tried, what "good" looks like — change results more than any clever phrasing.

**Show the Output You Want**
One worked example outperforms three paragraphs of description. You will practice giving examples, specifying format and length, and asking for the response you need the first time.

**Iterate Instead of Restarting**
Rather than rewriting from scratch, learn to push a response: "shorter," "more formal," "just the steps." Marie demonstrates a full iteration loop from vague request to usable result in four turns.

## Who Should Attend

Anyone who uses AI for writing, planning, research, or code and wants steadier results. Beginner-friendly, with plenty for experienced users to sharpen.

*"The model is not the variable you cannot control. Your clarity is the variable you can."* — Marie Dubois
