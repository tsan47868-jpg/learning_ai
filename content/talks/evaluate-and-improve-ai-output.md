Priya Sharma teaches the skill that separates AI users from AI professionals: judging output systematically instead of shrugging at it. If you have ever thought "this is… fine, I guess" — this session gives you a better answer than guesswork.

## What You'll Learn

**Define "Good" Before You Test**
Priya shows how to turn a vague wish ("the summary should be good") into checkable criteria: coverage, accuracy, tone, length. Ten minutes of definition saves hours of re-rolling and hoping.

**Build a Tiny Test Set**
You will collect ten real inputs and record what a correct output looks like for each. This becomes your personal benchmark — the same idea professional teams use, sized for one person.

**Spot the Failure Patterns**
Priya catalogs the recurring ways models fail — confident wrong facts, drifted format, ignored instructions, sycophantic agreement — and the specific check that catches each one.

**Iterate With Evidence**
Armed with failures, you will fix prompts where it actually helps, add examples where wording is the problem, and accept when the model is simply the wrong tool for the job.

## Who Should Attend

Practical AI users who ship real work and want to trust it more — writers, analysts, developers, and team leads. Beginner-friendly statistics-free approach.

*"You cannot improve what you have never measured. And you do not need a lab to measure ten examples."* — Priya Sharma
