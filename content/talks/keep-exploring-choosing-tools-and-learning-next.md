The AI tool landscape changes monthly, and Priya Sharma's final session is about navigating it without drowning. Learn to evaluate new tools calmly, decide what is worth adopting, and build a learning routine that keeps up without burning you out.

## What You'll Learn

**Filter the Noise**
New models and tools launch constantly, each with a demo that looks miraculous. Priya shares a durable filter — start from the task you have, not the feature being marketed — that survives every release cycle.

**A One-Page Tool Evaluation**
You will practice a quick scorecard: does it fit your actual task, what does it cost, where does your data go, and can you leave easily? Five questions that end most "should we adopt this?" debates.

**Adopt Deliberately**
Priya shows how to trial a tool safely on something unimportant, decide with a real week of use instead of first impressions, and keep an exit path so no single tool owns your workflow.

**A Learning Routine That Lasts**
Finally: how professionals keep current — a handful of trusted sources, one project in progress at all times, and the discipline of ignoring the rest without guilt.

## Who Should Attend

Anyone completing the learning path who wants to continue independently. Ideal as a closing session — you leave with a scorecard, a routine, and a next step.

*"The goal was never to know every tool. It is to never be lost when the tools change."* — Priya Sharma
